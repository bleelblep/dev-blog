## New glyph toy.

when I first got my nothing phone 3 like a week ago I basically installed all the apps that were made for the Glyph Matrix. So the last 2 days or so I've been playing the game "Glyph Bike" and I love it.

I love it so much it inspired me to try and implement a game into my glyph buddy app. At first I was thinking about implementing Tetris but I didn't think the limitations would make it an enjoyable experience. I then thought about a claw machine game but again during the planning stage I didn't know if I could make it graphically pleasing with the LED limitations.

## What did you choose?

I eventually settled on "UFO Abductor." It's a similar concept to the claw machine game but the visuals work great even with the LED limitations. 

it's a very simple game. there's a UFO that moves left to right. your job is to abduct the little pixels at the bottom of the screen which represent targets (that each have their own point value) to trigger the tractor beam you long press the glyph button.

you get 3 lives and the UFO gets faster as you continue through the levels. 


## Why wasn't it worth it
 
honestly it's a good simple idea for a game but I shouldn't have implemented it at this stage. I still have to fix the issues with weather and glyph visualizer. there are probably a few bugs with the quick settings too. on top of all of that I'm still tweaking the ui and color themes.

i should have polished off what I already had and not add onto a pile of spaghetti code. but we're past that stage.

## What's next?

I want to squash the existing bugs in Glyph Visualizer and Weather. In the Visualizer screen the bug where choosing spiral would stay enabled even if you pick an option on the bottom row has come back.

weather provider needs a better today/ tomorrow / day after indicator.  and overall more testing is needed.

about screen needs updating specifically re including Bluetooth into the relevant sections. onboarding needs to be redone as well.

then and only then should I touch the UFO game.

# What's the game status?

I mean I think it works. more testing is obviously needed but during my personal use it seems to be in a solid place. most of the features work and the overall visual look is nice and I feel very polished. the UFO beam needs to be slightly tweaked. maybe the sounds could be improved but I think it's a solid base.

id also like to continue adjusting the app ui. I think it's basically finished and I probably won't do anymore massive redesigns. I just need to do a few tweaks mostly with spacing and fonts.
