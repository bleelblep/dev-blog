Now for a completely unreleated topic but but something im very very interested in k-pop.

## K-pop really?

I know i know but i've been a kpop fan since around 2010 or 2011 when i stumbled onto T.O.P solo song turn it which turned me into a **HUGE** YG Entertainment fan, specifically 2NE1 and Big Bang.

I'll be the first to admin im very much a Gen 2 fan and in my opinion modern k-pop has transformed significantly since then in a way i personally dont like.

# Who is Teddy?

For newer fans Teddy Park was a producer for YG Entertainment he did a majority of 2NE1's discography and helped craft the 2010ish era of YG's Sound. 

He is now the founder of "The Black Label" and majority owner. Before that though he basically crafted the early sound of BlackPink. I'd say pretty much debut to pre "The Album" was all Teddy or in house YG producers. 

Currently The Black Label is home to Meovv the girl group with child model Ella Gross. She was basically inseperable from BlackPink in her trainee years with numerous photos of her and Jennie specifcally. They're also home to the "Samsung Chaebol Co-Ed Group" All Day Project. Think of a chaebol as an heiress like Paris Hilton. Theyre some of the biggest familys that all but own south korea. Then we've got Jeon Somi, Taeyang, Rose and Vince. They basically have some of the who's who's of kpop and basically every idol is a alumi from YG Entertainment.

# What is YG Entertainment?

Most people would be very family with the song Gangnam Style which was arguably K-pops first global breakout hit. Well the artist PSY was at the time under the label YG Entertainment who focus primarily on Hip-Hop and R&B sounds. In SEA (South East Asia) they housed some of the biggest groups in their era (BigBang and 2NE1.) They are also the label who houses BlackPink's grouo activites. They used to handle the members solo activites but all members bar rose have gone and done their own labels.

To fully understand YG's impact on k-pop they were considered one of the big 3 labels, the others being SM and JYP. Hell even BTS were and are fanboys of BigBang with some members citing BigBang as their inspiration to become K-pop idols.

Then theres G Dragon or Ji Yong (Goat or the living legend iykyk) before gangnam style he was being invited to global fashion shows. He was the person who went from idol to a legitimate cultural icon of korea. 

Its safe to say that some of the most iconic sounds and idols have all been part of YG at one point or another.

# Teddy Effect?

Hate it or love it Teddy Park defined the sound of YG for years. His style was so impactful that Lady Gaga tried to get him to produce for her (he said no for various reasons i won't get into.) His sound is unique and for some reason the general korean public absolutely eat it up, they love it.

# All Day Project (The point of this post)

Now we come to the actual point of this post All Day Project who have become 2025s breakout group in South Korea. The members are

- Annie (Chaebol) the granddaughter of Lee Myung He. the current chairwoman of the Samsung group
- Bailey a k-pop choreographier who has done psycho, illusion, savage, shoong and much much more.
- Youngseo who trained with NewJeans and was in the predebut lineup for Illit before leaving.
- Woochan a trainee under cube and bighit, who was also the youngest finalist on Show Me The Money at 13 years old.

and then we come to Lee Chaewon aka Tarzzan the controversial member of the group. He was previously a famous model and danced in south korea.

# I don't understand,  whats the big deal?

Before they even debuted All Day Project was born to be successful, with all the members being famous in their own right. Add one of K-pops most famous producers and you have a literal golden egg.

The issue seems to be aroound tarzzan who seems to be a very polarizing individual. The korean public absolutely love him which probably helped them become "Monster Rookies." The west absolutely loathe him and thats probably why theyre very very unpopular abroad, at least currently.

# Why this is interesting to me.

All Day Project were destined to succeed. There was literally no way they couldnt and imo every song release has been absolutely solid and a hit yet somehow theyre failing abroad (and quite badly too.) YG and by extensions The Black Label are very unique in K-pop. They have a very distinct style that is heavily heavily westernized especially compared to other labels. This makes them very popular and nearly everyone in south korea has opinions on either the trainees, the groups, their songs, the producers or just in general. 

What blows my mind is the polarizing popularity of All Day Project. And theres a very clear like between domestic and international popularity. Their songs are solid, the music videos are amazing and unique so i dont understand why they arent more popular internationally.

BlackPink, Rose, Taeyang, BigBang, G Dragon all have connections to Teddy and are all huge huge names but it seems the west has decided All Day Project is a nogo before thet even start.

# Why?

Theres probably numerous reasons behind this but if i were a betting man id place money on at least one of those reasons being tarzzan. Just google him and youll see why. 

Honestly it hurts me a little and i hope the international reception warms up to them. They're an amazing group with absolutely **HUGE** potential.
